If you want to edit your Duolingo username or email address, go to your settings and edit the username or email address. Your username appears on your weekly leaderboard. Remember to tap "Save changes" when you make any changes. If it is not changing, it means it is already taken by another Duolingo account. All usernames and email addresses are unique.
